Privacy

Your CV data

When you use "Generate with AI", the facts you entered are sent to our server, processed in memory to generate the rewritten CV, and returned to your browser. CV content is not stored on our server, not logged, and not used for AI training. Our server logs contain only timestamps and request statuses. Everything else in the app (the live preview, PDF and Word export) runs entirely in your browser.

Licence keys

If you paste a licence key, it is kept in your browser tab's memory only — never in localStorage or cookies — and sent to our server solely to validate your subscription.

Payments

Payments and subscriptions are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. We never see your card details. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy.
