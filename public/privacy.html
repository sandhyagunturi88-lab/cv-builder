Privacy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This is CV Builder, run by Cardzo Entertainment LLP. Here's what happens to your information when you use it.

What happens to my CV?

Most of the app runs on your own device — typing your details, previewing your CV, saving it as a PDF or Word file. None of that reaches our server.

Only when you press an "AI" button (like "Generate with AI") do we send your typed text to our server. We hold it in memory just long enough to get a reply, send that straight to your browser, then forget it — nobody looks through it or saves a copy.

Your progress also saves automatically, but only inside your own browser ("local storage"). It stays on this device until you clear it.

On a shared computer — school library, college IT room? Click "Clear & start over" before you log off, or the next person could see your CV.

We don't use cookies either — nothing tracks you between visits.

Who else sees my data?

Two other companies help us run this:

  • Anthropic makes the AI that writes and checks your CV. Your text goes to them to get a reply. They say it's never used to train their models, and kept only briefly, in case it's needed to check for abuse. Their privacy policy.
  • Lemon Squeezy handles payment if you subscribe, as "merchant of record" — they take your card details directly, so we never see or store them. Their privacy policy.
  • Render hosts the server this app runs on.

Licence keys

If you subscribe, you get a licence key by email. Paste it in and it's normally remembered only while that browser tab stays open — close the tab and it's gone; just paste it in again next time. If you tick "Remember my key on this device", the key is saved in your own browser's storage on that device (never a cookie, never sent to our server for safe-keeping) until you untick the box or the key stops working. Don't tick it on a shared or school computer.

If you're under 16

UK law says a parent or guardian should agree to any subscription or payment — check with them before you subscribe. Everything else here works the same whatever your age: your details are processed in memory, nothing is stored on our server.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have rights over your own information — even though this app doesn't keep an "account" for you.

  • Asking what we hold: usually nothing — we don't keep a copy of your CV or answers once a reply's gone back to your browser.
  • Asking us to delete something: same reason, nothing to delete on our end. To delete your own copy: "Clear & start over," or clear your browser's site data.
  • Complaining: if we've handled your information wrongly, complain to the UK regulator, the ICO, at ico.org.uk.
